Sources & scoring

Two kinds of question

Recorded questions preserve the answer tiers published by the independent Krillion Answers companion. They are not a live connection to Krillion’s validator.

Our original questions use bounded fact sets from the references below. Their house rarity tiers use English word frequency as a rough familiarity guide. They are not measured player popularity or official Krillion scores. A designated gem is chosen from the rarer part of each set; a less familiar answer can still earn 85 rather than 100.

Fact sources

  • Country facts: mledoze/countries, under the Open Database License. We limit these questions to the 193 UN member states; the source’s Vatican City membership flag is corrected.
  • Periodic table: Bowserinator/Periodic-Table-JSON, under CC BY-SA 3.0. Classification questions follow that table.
  • Constellations: the IAU list via VizieR, distributed in Astropy under its BSD license.
  • Familiarity data: wordfreq 3.1.1 by Robyn Speer and contributors; extracted frequency data is under CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • Other bounded sets cite NASA, BIPM, NATO, FIDE, sports bodies, publishers and reference works. The question definitions and individual source links are available in the project’s data directory.

Corrections and updates

Alternate names and close spellings can suggest a recorded answer for confirmation. This does not invent extra valid answers. The importer retains older questions and adds new ones only when recorded answers are public. Automatic daily imports are currently paused because the companion site denied the hosted runner access.

The editable country-derived data is distributed under ODbL, element-derived data under CC BY-SA 3.0, and extracted frequency values under CC BY-SA 4.0; retained license texts and source snapshots are included in the repository. These data terms do not license third-party logos or artwork.

Counterline is independent and is not affiliated with Krillion.
